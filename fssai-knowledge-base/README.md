## Document Classification

Documents are classified into the following categories:
- **REGULATION**: Primary regulatory documents
- **COMPENDIUM**: Ready-reference compilations
- **AMENDMENT**: Amendments to existing regulations
- **GAZETTE_NOTIFICATION**: Official Gazette notifications
- **DIRECTION**: Official directions
- **GUIDELINE**: Guidelines and procedures
- **MANUAL**: Operational manuals
- **DRAFT**: Draft regulations
- **ADMINISTRATIVE**: Administrative documents

## Relevance Classification

Documents are classified by relevance to FoodGaurd:
- **CORE**: Directly relevant to food analysis
- **SPECIALIZED**: Specific food categories or regulations
- **SUPPORTING**: Supporting information
- **ADMINISTRATIVE**: Administrative documents
- **IRRELEVANT**: Not relevant to food analysis

4. Amendment relationships may require manual verification

## Usage

This knowledge base is designed to be imported into a PostgreSQL/Prisma database for use by the FoodGaurd food-analysis engine.
