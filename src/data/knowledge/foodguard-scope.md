# FoodGuard — What the Assistant Can and Cannot Do

## What the assistant can do

The FoodGuard assistant is a product-facts assistant grounded in the FoodGuard database and
curated FSSAI knowledge:

- Explain a product's ingredients, nutrition and FoodGuard safety assessment.
- Explain individual ingredients, including INS numbers and additive roles.
- Compare two products on nutrition and ingredient dimensions.
- Summarise your recent scan history.
- Find FoodGuard information about a product by barcode or name.
- Answer questions about FSSAI labelling, additives, claims, packaging and grievance channels.
- Draft a complaint report for a scanned product when you ask for one.

## What the assistant cannot do

- The assistant cannot file complaints, submit reports, or send anything to FSSAI on your behalf.
- It does not have access to the scan history of any other user.
- It does not know about products that are not in the FoodGuard database.
- It does not give medical advice or diagnose conditions; health statements are general
  information only.
- It does not guarantee the absence of adulteration; its analysis is based on the label data
  available to FoodGuard.

## How answers are grounded

Answers are grounded in the FoodGuard product database, the curated FSSAI knowledge base, and the
user's own scan history. When information is missing or the assistant is unsure, it says so and
does not guess. Regulatory answers cite their FSSAI source document.

## Scope and limits

FoodGuard's knowledge base currently covers Indian food regulation topics (FSS Act 2006,
labelling, additives, contaminants, packaging, claims, grievances). Questions outside this scope
are answered only from the product database, or the assistant explains that the topic is outside
its coverage. Analysis quality depends on the completeness of the product's declared label data.

## Privacy

Conversations are stored per user and are never shared across users. The assistant never reads
other users' data, and never writes to the product database.
