# FSSAI — Grievance Redressal and Consumer Complaint Channels

## How a consumer can report unsafe food

A consumer who believes a food product is unsafe, sub-standard, misbranded or adulterated can
report it through the FSSAI food safety grievance portal (foodsafety.fssai.gov.in), the FSSAI
helpline (1800-112-100), the FSSAI Twitter/Facebook channels, or by approaching the designated
State Food Safety Officer (FSO) or the Commissioner of Food Safety of their state.

## What to include in a complaint

A useful complaint includes: the product name, brand, batch/lot number, date of manufacture and
expiry (Best Before) date, the FSSAI license number on the label, photographs of the product and
label, the place and date of purchase, and a copy of the bill or receipt. The more complete the
information, the faster the inspection can proceed.

## The process after filing

The Food Safety Officer receives the complaint, may inspect the premises and draw official
samples under Section 38 of the FSS Act, and send them to a notified laboratory for analysis. If
the sample is found to be sub-standard or unsafe, the FBO faces prosecution before the
Adjudicating Officer, penalties, and possible compensation to the complainant. The complainant is
kept informed of the outcome through the portal.

## Consumer rights under the FSS Act

Consumers have the right to be informed of the nature, substance and quality of food through
labelling, the right to protection against unfair trade practices, and the right to compensation
for injury caused by unsafe food (Section 65). Consumers may also approach the Consumer Protection
Commission for damages, and may file a criminal complaint where the adulteration endangers life.

## What FoodGuard does and does not do

FoodGuard helps you understand a product's ingredients and label so you can make an informed
decision, and can prepare a draft complaint for you. FoodGuard does not file complaints on your
behalf; filing is always done by you through the official FSSAI channels listed above.
